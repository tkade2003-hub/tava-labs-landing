Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Tava Labs ("Tava Labs", "we", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on tavalabs.app (the "Site"). By using the Site, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your actions and preferences, keep your checkout secure, and help us understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it. Similar technologies include pixels, tags, and local storage, which we refer to collectively as "cookies" in this policy.

Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly necessary cookies. These are essential for the Site to function — for example, keeping items in your cart, processing checkout securely, and preventing fraud. The Site cannot operate properly without them, and they cannot be switched off.

Functional cookies. These remember choices you make (such as preferences you set during our skin quiz) to provide a more personal experience.

Analytics and performance cookies. These help us count visits and understand how visitors move around the Site, so we can measure and improve performance. The information collected is aggregated and anonymous where possible.

Marketing and advertising cookies. These may be set by us or our advertising partners to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant ads on other sites. They do not store directly personal information but are based on uniquely identifying your browser and device.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages or process transactions for us — for example, our payment processor (Stripe) for secure checkout, our hosting provider, and analytics or advertising partners. These third parties may use cookies in accordance with their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

How to Control Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings — you can block or delete cookies, or set your browser to alert you when cookies are being sent. The "Help" section of your browser will explain how. Please note that disabling cookies may affect how the Site works: some features, including checkout, may not function properly.

Some browsers transmit a "Do Not Track" signal. Because there is no common industry standard for responding to these signals, the Site does not currently respond to them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
