The Night Shift: What Your Skin Does While You Sleep

Night is when skin does its repair work. Here is what is happening while you sleep — and how a 60-second evening routine backs it up.

You clock out. Your skin clocks in. The hours you spend asleep are when skin shifts into maintenance mode — and what you put on it before bed decides how much support that shift gets.

Skin works the night shift

During the day, skin is defensive: sunscreen, pollution, dry office air, blue light, stress. At night the priorities flip. Blood flow to the skin rises, moisture loss peaks, and the surface focuses on renewal. That is why you can go to bed looking fine and wake up looking like the meeting ran long — overnight is when dehydration shows.

It is also why the evening application matters as much as the morning one. Twice-daily use is not marketing math; it matches the two different jobs skin is doing.

The 60-second evening routine

  • Cleanse the day off — sunscreen, sweat, city air.
  • Serum: 2–4 pumps of Glow Brightening Serum on clean, dry skin, face and neck.
  • Moisturizer over the top to seal in the hydration while you sleep.
  • Sleep. Aim for a consistent window; skin keeps the same schedule you do.

Hydration is the night job

The serum's hydration stack — sodium hyaluronate, glycerin, sodium PCA and panthenol — is built for exactly this shift: drawing in water and helping skin hold onto it through the driest hours of the day. PDRN and peptide support the radiant, even look you want to wake up to.

Tava Labs started with a simple idea: know your score, fix your sleep, build the routine around it. The night routine is where that idea lives. Sixty seconds, then let the night shift do its work.
