Start Slow, Glow Steady: Your First 30 Days on the Serum

New serum, smart ramp. How the first month is structured — what to expect in week one, week two, and the day-30 mirror check.

Every good plan has a ramp. Training plans have one. Business plans have one. Skin deserves the same respect — because the fastest way to quit a serum is to overwhelm your skin in week one and blame the bottle.

Here is how the first 30 days on the Glow Brightening Serum are structured, and why.

Week 1: Introduce

Start once a day, in the evening, on clean dry skin. This lets your skin meet the formula — PDRN, peptide, hyaluronic acid, panthenol — without changing everything at once. Most people feel the hydration difference first: skin that feels comfortable instead of tight.

Week 2: Build

Move to twice daily — morning and night, 2–4 pumps each time. This is the rhythm the serum is designed for, and the rhythm the Tava plan tracks for you: morning application before moisturizer and sunscreen, evening application before your night cream.

Weeks 3–4: Steady state

By now the routine should feel automatic — two minutes, twice a day, no thinking. This is the window where consistent users typically start describing their skin as more even-looking and radiant: the glow is a cumulative effect, not an overnight event, and it compounds with sleep, water and sunscreen.

What to expect — honestly

  • Days 1–7: hydration and comfort. Skin feels softer, looks less dull after cleansing.
  • Days 8–21: texture feels smoother; the morning mirror looks more rested.
  • Day 30: the check-in that matters — compare your day-1 photo. Radiance and even tone are gradual, and photos catch what daily mirrors miss.

Start slow. Glow steady. The plan in the app walks the same ramp with you, day by day.
