Look Sharp by 9 AM: The Two-Minute Morning Routine

No ten-step routine. No guesswork. The exact two-minute morning sequence behind the Glow Brightening Serum — built for people whose calendar starts early.

Most skincare routines fail for one reason: they were designed for people with empty mornings. If your first call is at nine and your day is stacked after that, a routine survives only if it is fast, simple and impossible to get wrong.

Here is the whole system. Two minutes, four moves.

The routine

  • 0:00 — Rinse or cleanse. Lukewarm water is enough for most mornings; use a gentle cleanser if your skin runs oily.
  • 0:30 — Serum. 2–4 pumps of Glow Brightening Serum, pressed over face and neck on clean, dry skin. It absorbs fast — no greasy residue, no waiting around.
  • 1:30 — Moisturizer. A thin layer to lock in the hydration stack.
  • 1:45 — Sunscreen. Every day. It protects everything the other steps are building.

Why the order matters

Thinnest to thickest. A serum is water-based and concentrated, so it goes on first, directly against the skin where its ingredients — PDRN, acetyl hexapeptide-8, sodium hyaluronate, panthenol — can do their work. Cream and sunscreen sit on top as the seal and the shield.

The real point: a routine you keep

The best routine is the one still running on day 60. That is why the serum was made to be the single step the whole morning is built around — one bottle, twice a day, with the Tava app there to keep the streak, the reminders and the plan in one place.

Two minutes. Then go build the day.
