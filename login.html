Your Tava account

Sign in

Use the same email you bought with or use in the Tava mobile app — your plan, orders, and routine are tied to that email, so everything shows up here exactly as it does in the app. No password: we email you a 6-digit code and a sign-in link, and either one signs you in.

Bought on the web, using the app?

Use your claim code in the app

When you bought through the quiz or this site, your purchase confirmation email (and the page right after checkout) showed a claim code. Open the Tava mobile app, sign in, and enter that code in the Orders tab — your purchase, plan, and quiz answers move into your app account, no second purchase.

On the web it's even simpler: sign in above with the same email you checked out with and your orders and plan are already here.
