Shipping Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Shipping Policy explains how and when orders from Tava Labs (tavalabs.app) are processed and delivered. Tava Labs is an online-only store; all orders are placed through our website and shipped to the address you provide at checkout.

Order Processing Time

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days (Monday–Friday, excluding US holidays) after payment is confirmed. You will receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking information once your order has shipped. Orders placed on weekends or holidays begin processing on the next business day.

Delivery Estimates

We currently ship within the United States only. Standard delivery typically arrives within 3–5 business days after your order ships. Delivery times are estimates provided by our carriers and are not guaranteed; actual transit times may vary by destination and carrier conditions.

Shipping Rates

Shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on your delivery address and the items in your order, and are shown before you complete your purchase. Any free-shipping promotions will be displayed at checkout when your order qualifies.

Tracking Your Order

When your order ships, we email tracking information to the address you provided at checkout. Please allow up to 24 hours for tracking to update after you receive the shipping confirmation. If your tracking shows delivered but you have not received your package, check with neighbors and your local carrier first, then contact us.

Shipping Address Accuracy

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. We are not responsible for orders delivered to an incorrect or incomplete address provided by the customer. If you notice an error, email us at talia@tavalabs.app as soon as possible — if the order has not yet shipped, we will do our best to correct it. Packages returned to us as undeliverable can be reshipped once the address is confirmed; additional shipping charges may apply.

Delays and Damaged Shipments

Occasionally, deliveries are delayed by carrier disruptions, weather, or other events outside our control. If your order is significantly delayed, contact us and we will help. If your order arrives damaged, please see our Return & Refund Policy — contact us within 14 days of delivery and we will make it right.

Subscription Shipments

Subscription orders ship automatically on your renewal schedule. Each renewal is processed and shipped under the same timelines described above, and you will receive a tracking email for every shipment. You can pause, skip, or cancel your subscription at any time before a renewal is processed.

Contact Us

Questions about shipping? Email us at talia@tavalabs.app.
