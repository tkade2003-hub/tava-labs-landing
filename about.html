About Us

Skincare for the ambitious.

Build your business. Do not neglect yourself.

Your appearance is part of your professional presence. Tava Labs is a personal maintenance brand for people building businesses. We make one product and we make it well. The Glow Brightening Serum is a lightweight daily serum built for founders and entrepreneurs who pitch investors and sell on calls and record content and meet partners, and who understand that how they present themselves matters.

Building a company asks everything of you, and the long days show on your face first. People read your energy and your composure before they finish hearing your idea. This serum is the daily preparation step that helps you look prepared and composed and polished, in the room and on camera. Looking ready is not vanity. It is part of presenting the business well.

The Serum

Glow Brightening Serum · 1 fl oz (30ml)

  • PDRN and acetyl hexapeptide-8 — for a more luminous, vibrant-looking complexion
  • Sodium hyaluronate (hyaluronic acid), glycerin, and sodium PCA — deep hydration for plump, soft-looking skin
  • Panthenol and propanediol — conditioning for a silky, comfortable feel
  • Lightweight and fast-absorbing, with no greasy residue — made in the USA, for all skin types
  • Use twice daily: 2 to 4 pumps on clean, dry skin, morning and night

The daily complexion routine. One step in the morning and one step at night, made to fit a busy working day without a long grooming routine.
